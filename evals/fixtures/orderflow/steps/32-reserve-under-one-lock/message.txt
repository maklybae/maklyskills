inventory: hold and release stock under one store lock

Reserve read every level, checked it, and wrote it back with SetLevel, so two
reservations that each fit on their own could both pass the check and take the
shelf below zero. We oversold the last monitor arm twice this month.

The check and the write now happen inside one store call under one lock. The
regression test runs forty single item reservations against ten on hand and
expects exactly ten of them to be accepted.
