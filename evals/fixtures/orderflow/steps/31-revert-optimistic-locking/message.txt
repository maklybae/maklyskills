Revert "orders: guard the memory store with optimistic locking"

This reverts commit __REVERT_SHA__.

The version check bought less than it cost. A lost update needs two writes to
the same order within a few milliseconds, which here means the payment callback
and the warehouse touching one order in the same second; that has happened twice
since September. The retry loop it forced on callers deadlocked every couple of
days instead: two callers reloading each other's orders, both retrying, neither
getting through.

Keep the single mutex until the store is something other than a map in this
process.
